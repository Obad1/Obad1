HUMAN REVIEW

How findings are checked

Every flagged amount, date and reference is tied to its input row and checked by a human operator before delivery. Final accounting decisions are made by your accountant.

OUT OF SCOPE

Known exclusions

Split/partial settlements, multiple currencies, OCR from scans, statement balance certification, ERP posting and assurance opinions are excluded.

Status legend

MATCH = supplied values agree under the stated comparison rule; REVIEW = possible issue or supporting evidence requiring a person to confirm; OPEN = unresolved difference or missing information. None is an authorisation to post, pay, ship or attest.

Frequently asked questions

Why not just use Excel, an ERP or my accountant?

We focus on a narrowly scoped comparison and explain unresolved exceptions. We do not replace your existing tools or professional sign-off.

What if nothing is flagged?

We state the matching rules applied, the source coverage, and the limitations. No exceptions detected is not proof that every record is correct.

Can you review scanned files or other languages?

Not in the initial structured pilot. Unreadable scans, handwriting and unsupported formats need separate review and quotation.

How fast will you reply?

We aim to acknowledge genuine inquiries within one business day, subject to availability. The 48-hour sample clock starts only after acceptance of complete non-sensitive inputs and written confirmation.

How can I book a scoping call?

Send “15-minute scoping call” by email or WhatsApp with this service name and your time zone. We will confirm availability manually.
